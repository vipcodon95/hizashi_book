## Bài đọc 2 — Hướng dẫn: trả lời lời mời sao cho lịch sự

> **Yes, No or Late? How to Answer an Invitation**
>
> An invitation is a kind way of saying, "We would like to spend time with you." A quick, clear response is the best way to say thank you, even when the answer is no.
>
> **Answer on time**
>
> Many cards say RSVP, which comes from French and means "please reply." Reply by the date on the card, because the host has to plan the food and the chairs. Never say "maybe" and then forget about it.
>
> **Saying yes**
>
> Thank the host and accept clearly: "Yes, I'd love to come!" The invitation is usually only for the people on the card, so don't bring a friend without asking first.
>
> **Saying no**
>
> It's OK to say no. Apologize, give a short reason and, if you like, suggest another day to get together. You don't need a long story: "What a pity! I'm working that evening, but thank you" is enough.
>
> **Running late**
>
> Text the host before the party and give the time of your arrival. At a surprise party, arriving after the birthday person can spoil the big moment, so try to be on time.
>
> **Gifts and good manners**
>
> Customs are different from place to place and from family to family, so when you aren't sure, ask the host about gifts. If the card says "No presents," a card or a kind note is enough. Don't post photos of a surprise party online before the big moment, and send a short thank-you message the next day.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- **Câu tường thuật** lùi thì một bậc: `"I will blow up balloons"` → `Trevor said that he would blow up balloons`; `"I'm making…"` → `she was making…`; `"I can't come"` → `she couldn't come`.
- **say** không cần người nghe ngay sau (`Leah said that…`); **tell** phải có người nghe (`Tracy told me that…`, `Dad told Uncle Nathan that…`). **explain that** = giải thích rằng, thường đi kèm lý do.
- Đổi đại từ khi tường thuật: lời của Leah "I" thành `she`, lời của Ethan thành `he`. Câu hỏi "What did X say?": tìm tên X đứng trước said / told / explained.
- Thiệp mời có cấu trúc cố định (When / Where / Please reply by…), bài hướng dẫn có tiêu đề nhỏ: đọc câu hỏi trước, rồi tìm đúng dòng / đúng mục.
- Câu suy luận và câu nối hai bài: bài không nói thẳng. Ghép chi tiết nhỏ (ai có mặt ở đâu, ai hành động lạ), hoặc đặt lời nhân vật ở bài 1 cạnh lời khuyên ở bài 2 rồi so từng việc.
