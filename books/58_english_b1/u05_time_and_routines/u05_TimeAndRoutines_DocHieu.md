So I made a decision: from tomorrow, I'm going to do my homework in short time slots, and I'll reward myself with ten minutes of soccer videos after each one. Mom just smiled and said, "I'll believe it when I see it." We'll see who's right by Friday!

## Bài đọc 2 — Kỹ thuật Pomodoro

> **Work in Tomatoes: The Pomodoro Technique**
>
> Do you sit down to study and then find yourself checking your phone twenty minutes later? Many students have the same problem, and a simple method from the late 1980s may help. It was created by Francesco Cirillo, an Italian university student who had trouble paying attention to his books. He used a kitchen timer in the shape of a tomato, and "pomodoro" is the Italian word for tomato.
>
> The method is easy. First, choose one task, such as a reading or a set of math problems. Then set a timer for twenty-five minutes and work on that task only. When the timer rings, take a short break of about five minutes: stand up, drink some water or look out of the window. During a pomodoro, turn off your notifications and keep your phone in another room. Each twenty-five-minute period is called a "pomodoro." After four pomodoros, you take a longer break of fifteen to thirty minutes.
>
> Why does it work? A deadline that is only twenty-five minutes away feels immediate, so it is harder to put your work off. Short, frequent breaks also stop you from getting exhausted, and many people say that they feel more productive by the end of the day.
>
> However, the technique is not perfect for every job. Some tasks, such as writing a long story, need longer periods of deep thinking, and some people find the ticking timer stressful. Others prefer to work in fifty-minute slots with ten-minute breaks. The best advice is to try it for a week and then decide if it suits you.

- B. shouting for help on Sunday nights *(hét lên nhờ giúp tối Chủ nhật)*
- C. working in short time slots with a reward after each one *(làm bài theo từng khoảng ngắn, xong mỗi khoảng được thưởng)*
- D. finishing his history project within five days *(làm xong dự án sử trong năm ngày)*

---

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Câu hỏi ý chính / tiêu đề: đọc câu đầu và câu cuối mỗi đoạn trước. Các phương án sai thường là một chi tiết nhỏ có trong bài (cà chua, điện thoại).
- Nhiều mốc thời gian trong một câu (`by`, `prior to`, `within`): nối từng việc với từng mốc trước khi chọn.
- Câu hỏi từ trong ngữ cảnh: tìm manh mối ngay cạnh từ đó (`until the last minute` giúp đoán `put off`).
- Câu NOT / EXCEPT: đánh dấu ba phương án CÓ trong bài; phương án còn lại là đáp án.
- Câu nối hai bài: tìm ý giống nhau (khoảng làm việc ngắn + nghỉ) chứ không tìm từ giống nhau.
