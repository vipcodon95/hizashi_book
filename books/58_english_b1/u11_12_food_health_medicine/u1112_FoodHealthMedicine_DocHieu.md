## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 11) — Tháng Một ăn lành mạnh của bố

> **Dad's Healthy January**
>
> Every January, my dad makes a promise, and this year it was a big one: "No more takeaway pizza for a month!" He said that cooking at home is cheaper and healthier, and that eating together is the best part of the day. Trevor lost his appetite as soon as he heard it. He said that he could happily live on pizza, but Mom reminded him that a good diet needs the right proportion of vegetables.
>
> On the first night, Dad tried out a vegetarian curry with spinach, ginger and lots of spices. It was so spicy that Trevor drank three glasses of milk, but I loved the flavor. The next evening, Dad grilled salmon and served it with plain rice and broccoli. Mom said that salmon is full of nutrients that are good for your heart and brain. Trevor asked if there was a substitute for broccoli. "Yes," Dad said, "it's called more broccoli."
>
> Dad also started reading the ingredients on every package, and he was shocked by how much sugar some sauces contain. After that, he cut out sugar from his coffee, and he was in a bad mood until lunch the next day. Not every meal was a success. Dad's cabbage soup was so tasteless that even Biscuit, who usually wants everything, did not come into the kitchen. Cooking every night is also tiring, so on busy days we used frozen vegetables, which are often just as nutritious as fresh ones.
>
> In thirty days, we did not order takeaway food even once. Mom felt more energetic, and Trevor admitted that he likes salmon now. My job was to stir the sauces, and I've decided that I want to be a chef. Next January, I'm going to choose the promise!
>
> Tina

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 12) — Cảm lạnh, cúm và kháng sinh

> **Colds, Flu and Antibiotics: What You Should Know**
>
> Every winter, our emergency room fills up with people who have come down with a cold or the flu. Many of them ask for antibiotics, but these medicines do not work against viruses, which cause colds and the flu. Antibiotics only treat infections caused by bacteria, such as some ear infections. Taking them when you do not need them will not help you get over a cold faster. Worse, using antibiotics too often helps bacteria become resistant, so the medicine may not work when you really need it.
>
> So what ought you to do? For most colds, rest, plenty of water and time are the best treatment, and most people recover within about seven to ten days. The flu is usually more serious, and its symptoms, such as fever, aches and feeling very tired, often come on suddenly. Getting a flu vaccine every year is the best way to protect yourself, and doctors recommend it for almost everyone six months and older.
>
> However, see a doctor quickly if you have trouble breathing, chest pain or a fever that does not go away, or if you feel better and then suddenly worse. "Coming to the emergency room for a simple cold is rarely necessary," says Dr. Leah Carter, a first-year resident at Sunset Bay. "But please do not stay at home when you are really unwell. We would rather see you too early than too late."
>
> Finally, wash your hands often, and stay away from work or school when you are sick to avoid passing the virus to others.
>
> Sunset Bay Hospital

- C. Simple daily habits, such as eating well and resting, are important for health. *(Những thói quen hằng ngày giản dị, như ăn uống tốt và nghỉ ngơi, rất quan trọng với sức khoẻ.)*
- D. Hospitals should cook healthier food. *(Bệnh viện nên nấu món lành mạnh hơn.)*

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `so … that …` = đến mức mà. Câu hỏi "Why…?" thường có đáp án là vế trước `that`.
- Câu nói đùa của nhân vật ("it's called more broccoli"): đừng hiểu theo nghĩa đen. Hãy hỏi người đó thật sự muốn nói gì.
- Tờ thông tin sức khoẻ: tách rõ *khi nào tự chăm sóc ở nhà* và *khi nào phải đi khám*. Câu áp dụng tình huống luôn hỏi vào ranh giới này.
- Tránh phương án tuyệt đối (`never`, `every`, `always`) khi bài nói `rarely`, `usually`, `often`.
