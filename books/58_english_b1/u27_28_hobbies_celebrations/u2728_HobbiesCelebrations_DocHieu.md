## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 27) — Nhật ký học đàn ukulele của Megan

> **Megan's Ukulele Diary**
>
> Week 1
>
> I've finally done it: I've signed up for a beginners' ukulele workshop at the community center. I remember hearing my grandfather play when I was little, and I've always wanted to try. Our instructor, a cheerful man called Gus, says that the ukulele is the friendliest instrument in the world: it has only four strings, and it's small and cheap. Most of the other students are retired, and one of them, a woman of eighty, played in a band when she was young. After two hours, my fingertips hurt, and I could play exactly two chords.
>
> Week 3
>
> This week, I nearly gave up. I work night shifts, and after twelve hours at the hospital, practicing is the last thing I want to do. Then I tried playing for just ten minutes a day instead of an hour on weekends, and it worked much better! Now I practice before I leave for work, and I keep the ukulele next to the coffee machine, so I can't forget about it. Gus also told us to stop looking at our fingers. "Your hands know more than you think," he said.
>
> Week 6
>
> Big progress: I can play a whole song now, slowly.
>
> On Tuesday, I was practicing in the kitchen when Tina stopped to listen. Then she grabbed a wooden spoon and started singing into it like a microphone. Mitchell says that I've stopped singing in the shower, because now I sing everywhere else in the house. Trevor, who is usually very honest, said, "Not bad, Mom." From a teenager, that's a huge compliment.
>
> Week 8
>
> Note to myself: remember to bring along my tuner on Thursday, and don't forget to change the strings. Gus says that old strings never stay in tune. Learning something new in my forties is challenging, but it makes me feel like a student again, and that's a feeling that I had forgotten.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 28) — Thông báo hội chợ mùa thu Maple Street

> **Maple Street Fall Fair**
>
> Our annual fall fair is back, and this year it's bigger than ever!
>
> Date: Saturday, October 24
>
> Time: 11 a.m. – 8 p.m.
>
> Place: Pine Street Park
>
> There will be more than twenty food stalls, pumpkin carving for kids, and a riddle competition run by the book club at Harriet's. The food stalls will sell everything from hot dogs to apple pie, and most dishes will cost less than $8. A magician will perform at 4 p.m., and our folk music stage will have an open hour at 5 p.m. for beginners: bring along your instrument and play one song.
>
> Don't miss the "Harvest Around the World" tent, where neighbors share harvest traditions from the places where their families come from. Last year, visitors learned how one family celebrates the end of the rice harvest in their grandparents' village, and how another family still makes apple juice every fall, just as they did on their old farm. Every family does it differently, and that's the fun of it.
>
> Rules for the day
>
> Stall holders are to arrive by 9:30 a.m. and are to take all their trash home with them. No fireworks are to be let off in the park; the evening light show will use lanterns and lights only. Dogs are welcome, but they are to be kept on a leash. Children under 10 are to stay with an adult at all times.
>
> At 7:30 p.m., we will give away all the pumpkins that are left, so bring a bag! We still need volunteers to help put up the tents in the morning and to pick up trash in the afternoon. Even one hour helps. We would like to show our gratitude to Harriet's for the free coffee, and to everyone who has offered to help. Want to join them? Contact Helena Brooks at the Maple Street Neighbors Group.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `remember + V-ing` = nhớ việc đã làm; `remember + to V` = nhớ để làm. Tương tự với `forget`.
- `stop + V-ing` = thôi làm; `stop + to V` = dừng việc khác để làm. `try + V-ing` = thử xem sao; `try + to V` = cố gắng.
- `be + to V` trong thông báo = quy định bắt buộc, nghĩa gần với must. `are not to` = không được.
- Câu áp dụng quy định: tìm con số hoặc điều kiện (`under 10`, `by 9:30`) rồi so với người trong câu hỏi.
