## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 13) — Email của Tracy kể chuyến trượt tuyết

> **Subject: Our ski trip (please don't laugh!)**
>
> Hi Leah,
>
> You asked about my long weekend in the mountains, so here is the whole story.
>
> Four of us had planned to set out at six on Saturday morning, because the ski resort was approximately four hours from campus. Unfortunately, Jenna's alarm hadn't gone off, so we didn't leave until nine. Then, two hours into the drive, we ran out of gas on a quiet mountain road. Nobody had checked the tank before we left, which was absolutely unbelievable for three engineering students. Owen, a friend of Jenna's from my math class, walked almost a mile to a gas station and came back with a can of gas and four hot chocolates.
>
> By the time we arrived, the ski school had closed for the day. Worse, the owner of our cabin had accidentally given it to another group, and he apologized again and again. We eventually found a small, old motel near the road and spent the evening playing cards.
>
> On Sunday, everything changed. We got up early and watched the sunrise from the top of the mountain, and the view was incredible. I had never skied before, and the slope looked very steep to me. I fell about twenty times, but Owen stayed with me all morning and showed me how to stop and turn. He never laughed at me, not even once. By lunchtime, I could ski down the easy slope without falling.
>
> We packed up and headed for home at four, and this time we filled the tank first! It wasn't a perfect trip, but it will be a lasting memory.
>
> P.S. Owen and I are going to get coffee next week. Please don't say anything to my mom yet!
>
> Tracy

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 14) — Đường mòn Pacific Crest Trail

> **The Pacific Crest Trail: 2,650 Miles on Foot**
>
> The Pacific Crest Trail, or PCT, is one of the longest hiking trails in the United States. It runs approximately 2,650 miles from the Mexican border in Southern California to the Canadian border in Washington State. Along the way, it crosses deserts, forests and high mountains in three states: California, Oregon and Washington.
>
> Many people walk short sections of the trail, but every year some hikers try to walk its full length in one season. Most of these hikers start in the south in April or May and need about five months to reach the end. If they start too early, there is still deep snow in the Sierra Nevada mountains, and if they start too late, they may not reach Washington before the winter snow arrives.
>
> The trail is not easy. In the desert sections, water can be very hard to find, so hikers often have to carry several liters with them. In the mountains, the paths are steep and rough, and storms can arrive suddenly. Hikers follow signposts and maps, but it is still possible to get lost, and sometimes they have to turn back because of snow or fire.
>
> Long-distance hikers spend months planning their trip. They need a permit, and they usually mail boxes of food to small towns along the trail, where they can pick them up on the way. A full backpack, with food, water and a tent, can easily weigh thirty pounds or more.
>
> Maya Holt, who walked the whole trail in 2023, says that the hardest part was not her body but her mind. "I had been walking for three weeks before my legs finally stopped hurting," she says. "But on bad days, I wanted to go home every hour. Then one morning, I stood on the edge of a mountain, looked at the distant hills, and knew that I was going to finish." When she eventually arrived safely at the Canadian border, she cried for ten minutes.
>
> If you want to try the PCT, start with a short section near your home. Some parts of the trail are only about an hour's drive from San Diego.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `By the time + quá khứ đơn, … had + V3`: việc ở vế had + V3 xảy ra TRƯỚC. Câu hỏi thứ tự sự việc hay hỏi vào đây.
- `had been + V-ing for + khoảng thời gian`: nhấn mạnh việc đã kéo dài bao lâu trước một mốc trong quá khứ.
- Tái bút (P.S.) trong email thường chứa điều người viết thật sự muốn nói. Câu hỏi về cảm xúc hay lấy bằng chứng ở đây.
- Chữ `this time` (lần này) ngầm so với lần trước. Hãy tìm lần trước trong bài để hiểu vì sao.
