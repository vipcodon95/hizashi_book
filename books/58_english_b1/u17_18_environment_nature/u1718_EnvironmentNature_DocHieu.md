## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 17) — Bài báo trường của Trevor về vườn của câu lạc bộ Xanh

> **From Dirt to Dinner: The Green Club's New Garden**
>
> Written by: Trevor Carter
>
> Last fall, the area behind our gym was just dry dirt and a few dead bushes. Every night, the sprinklers on the sports field were left on for hours, and most of the water simply flowed into the street. Mr. Dawson, our science teacher, called it "a waste of water in a city that doesn't get much rain", and he asked the Green Club to do something about it.
>
> First, we put four large barrels under the gym roof so that we could collect rainwater. It doesn't rain often in San Diego, but the numbers surprised us: one inch of rain on a roof of 1,000 square feet gives you about 600 gallons of water. Our barrels were full after the first storm in January, and we have used that water for the garden ever since. The school also agreed to water the sports field only in the early morning.
>
> Next, we sorted the cafeteria trash for one week so as to find out what students really throw out. The results were shocking: almost half of it could be recycled or turned into compost. We found hundreds of milk cartons, plastic straws and whole apples that nobody had touched. Now there are three bins in the cafeteria, and the food waste goes into a compost box next to the garden.
>
> In February, we planted tomatoes, herbs and native flowers that need very little water. The cafeteria cooks have promised to use our herbs in their soups this spring.
>
> To be honest, I only joined the club because my friend Jake did. Since I made the school soccer team at the start of March, I can only come to meetings on Wednesdays, but I still check the barrels every morning before class. If you want to get your hands dirty, the Green Club meets in Room 14 on Wednesdays at 3:15. Bring old clothes!

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 18) — Vì sao cần bảo vệ loài ong

> **Why Bees Need to Be Protected**
>
> Next time you eat an apple, an almond or a strawberry, you can thank a bee. When bees fly from flower to flower to collect food, they carry pollen, and this helps plants to make fruit and seeds. This process is called pollination, and about one in every three bites of the food we eat depends on animals like bees.
>
> Most people think of honeybees, which live together in large groups and make honey. However, honeybees are not originally from North America; they were brought here from Europe in the 1600s. In fact, there are about 4,000 kinds of native bees in the United States, and most of them live alone, not in large groups. Many build their nests in small holes in the ground or in dead wood. Some are so small that you might think they are flies.
>
> Sadly, bees face serious threats. When fields and gardens are covered with roads and buildings, bees lose their natural habitat. Chemicals that people use to kill insects can also kill bees, and disease and extreme weather make the problem worse. In recent years, beekeepers in the United States have lost a large part of their bees every year, and scientists worry that some native kinds could die out.
>
> The good news is that bees are easy to help, even in a small city garden. Plant flowers that grow naturally in your area, and choose different kinds so that bees can find food from spring to fall. Leave a small dish of water with some stones in it, so that bees can rest on the stones while they drink. Try not to use chemicals in the garden. And if you see a swarm of bees, don't try to kill them: they are usually calm because they are only looking for a new home, so call a local beekeeper, who can take them away safely.
>
> Bees need to be protected not only because they are amazing animals, but also because our food depends on them.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `so that + mệnh đề` và `so as to + V` đều chỉ mục đích. Câu hỏi "Why…?" hay có đáp án nằm ngay sau hai cụm này.
- `need to be + V3` (bị động nguyên mẫu) = cần được làm gì. Tiêu đề bài 2 nói luôn ý chính.
- Câu suy luận về chi tiết: hỏi "chi tiết này cho thấy điều gì?". Đừng chọn phương án bài không hề nói (vd "táo bị hỏng").
- Câu nối hai bài: tìm lời khuyên ở bài này, rồi tìm việc ở bài kia làm đúng lời khuyên đó.
