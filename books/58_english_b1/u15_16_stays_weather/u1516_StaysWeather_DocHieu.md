## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 15) — Chloe đánh giá nhà nghỉ ở Maui

> **Sea Breeze Guesthouse, Maui**
>
> Rating: 4 out of 5
>
> Reviewed by: Chloe B., San Diego
>
> I came to Maui for work, not a vacation: a small surf school had asked me to design a new logo and website, and they wanted me to see their beach first. The surf school couldn't offer me a room, so I booked into this family-run guesthouse for five nights. I had read both good and bad reviews, so I didn't know what to expect.
>
> First, the problems. My flight was held up, and I didn't arrive until after midnight. The reception desk had closed at ten, and nobody answered the phone. Luckily, the owner, Mrs. Kahale, had left my key in an envelope with a handwritten note: "Welcome! How about breakfast at eight?" The room was clean but a little dusty in the corners, and the bathroom light didn't work until the second day. There was neither a TV nor a desk, which was a disappointment, because I had planned to work in my room.
>
> But the guesthouse did not just live up to the good reviews; it was even better. Breakfast was not only delicious but also different every day: fresh pineapple, banana bread and the best coffee I've ever had. When I mentioned the desk, Mrs. Kahale gave me two options: I could either work at the big table in the garden or use the quiet room next to the kitchen. I chose the garden, and every morning I designed with a stunning ocean view in front of me. How beautiful it was!
>
> The atmosphere here is friendly and relaxing rather than fancy. If you want a luxury beach resort with a pool, this is not the right option for you. But if you want quiet, kind people and a place that feels like home, I'm happy to recommend it. I was pleasantly surprised, and I've already made a booking for my next trip.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 16) — Hiện tượng El Niño

> **El Niño: When the Ocean Changes the Weather**
>
> Every few years, something unusual happens in the Pacific Ocean. Near the equator, strong winds usually blow from east to west and push warm surface water toward Asia and Australia. During an El Niño, these winds become weaker, and the warm water spreads back east toward South America. The name is Spanish for "the little boy", and it was first used by fishermen in Peru, who noticed that the sea sometimes became warmer than usual.
>
> El Niño usually happens every two to seven years and lasts about nine to twelve months. Although it starts in one ocean, it affects the weather in many regions of the world. Australia and Indonesia often become drier, and some places there have serious fires. In Peru, heavy rainfall can cause floods, and fishermen catch fewer fish, because the warm water contains fewer nutrients. Over the Atlantic Ocean, there are usually fewer hurricanes.
>
> Here in Southern California, an El Niño winter is often wetter than usual. However, it does not always work that way. In the strong El Niño of 2015–2016, many people prepared for heavy storms, but Southern California got less rain than expected.
>
> If you lived in a fishing town in Peru, you would probably watch the ocean temperature as carefully as the weather forecast. Scientists now use satellites and floating instruments to measure the temperature of the sea, so they can often warn people several months ahead. The opposite pattern, called La Niña, brings cooler water to the same area, and it often makes Southern California drier.
>
> El Niño also raises global temperatures a little, so the hottest years on record often come during or just after one. Scientists are still studying how climate change might affect El Niño in the future.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Bài đánh giá thường có hai phần: điểm chưa tốt và điểm tốt. Câu hỏi ý chính cần phương án nói được cả hai.
- `neither A nor B` = không có cả A lẫn B. Câu hỏi NOT TRUE hay hỏi vào chỗ này.
- `If + quá khứ, … would …` (câu điều kiện loại 2) là tình huống giả định, không phải sự thật về người đọc.
- Phân biệt `every two to seven years` (bao lâu một lần) với `lasts nine to twelve months` (kéo dài bao lâu).
