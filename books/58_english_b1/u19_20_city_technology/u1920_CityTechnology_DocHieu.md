## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 19) — Phỏng vấn Helena về cây cầu mới

> **A New Bridge for Valley Springs**
>
> Neighborhood News reporter Corinne Whitfield talked to Helena Brooks, the engineer who led the project.
>
> **Corinne:** Helena, why did Valley Springs need a new bridge?
>
> **Helena:** The old one was built in the 1950s, when the town was much smaller. It was made of wood, and trucks heavier than ten tons weren't allowed on it, so farmers had to drive twenty-five miles around the hills to take their crops to market. And the town is growing. More and more young families are moving away from the city, because houses are cheaper and many people can work from home now.
>
> **Corinne:** What was the hardest part?
>
> **Helena:** Time. We had to start in the fall, which is the busiest season for farmers, and we couldn't close the road, because the whole town depends on it. Our solution to the problem was to build the new bridge right next to the old one, so the old one stayed open until the new one was ready. Then we pulled the old bridge down in just two weeks.
>
> **Corinne:** How long did it take, and how did local people feel?
>
> **Helena:** Fourteen months. At first, some people were worried about the noise and the traffic jams, and I understood their reason for complaining. But they were also incredibly kind. Every Friday, a farmer called Mr. Hollis brought my team a box of ripe peaches. On opening day, the children rode their bikes across first, and then Mr. Hollis drove his herd of cattle over after them. The whole town laughed.
>
> **Corinne:** You usually work on projects in San Diego. Which do you prefer?
>
> **Helena:** City projects are far bigger and more famous, but this one meant much more to me, because I could see how it changed people's lives. I'd love to live in a small town like that one day. My husband, Nathan, isn't so sure. He says he would only move if the internet there were faster!

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 20) — AI trong đời sống hằng ngày

> **AI in Everyday Life: A Useful Helper, Not a Perfect One**
>
> You probably use artificial intelligence, or AI, many times a day without thinking about it. When a map app warns you about a traffic jam, when spam is removed from your email, or when your phone makes a dark photo brighter, AI is doing the work. In the last few years, there has also been a big breakthrough: AI chat tools that can answer questions, write emails and even generate pictures in seconds.
>
> How do these tools work? They are trained on huge amounts of text, pictures and other information, and they learn patterns from it. They do not think like people, but they are very good at guessing what usually comes next. That is why they are so handy, and also why they sometimes make mistakes.
>
> An AI tool can give you an answer that sounds completely sure and is completely wrong. Nathan Brooks, a software engineer in San Diego, uses AI tools at work every day. "I was given a great tool, not a perfect one," he says. "It saves me hours, but I still check everything, especially numbers, dates and facts that matter."
>
> There are other questions too. Before you upload a photo or a document, think about who might see it, and never type passwords or bank details into a chat tool. Many people also wonder if AI will take over their jobs. Experts do not agree, but many think that AI will change a lot of jobs rather than simply remove them, and that people who know how to use it well will have an advantage.
>
> For students, the advice is simple: use AI to explain a difficult idea or to check your writing, but do your own thinking first. If a tool does your homework for you, you are given the answer but not the skill.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Bài phỏng vấn: câu hỏi của phóng viên chia bài thành từng phần. Dò câu hỏi trước để tìm nhanh đoạn chứa đáp án.
- `far / much + so sánh hơn` nhấn mạnh mức chênh lệch. `far bigger … but … much more to me` = lớn hơn nhiều nhưng ý nghĩa hơn hẳn.
- `I was given…` / `you are given…` = được trao, được đưa. Người nhận đứng làm chủ ngữ.
- Câu nối hai bài về một nhân vật: gom thông tin về người đó ở cả hai bài, rồi loại phương án gán nhầm việc của người khác.
