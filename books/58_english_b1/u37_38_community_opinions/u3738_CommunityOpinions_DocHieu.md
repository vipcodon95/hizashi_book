## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 37) — Bản tin cảm ơn đợt quyên góp áo ấm

> **Warm Coats for Winter: Thank You, Maple Street!**
>
> Maple Street Neighbors Group · December newsletter
>
> Three weeks ago, we asked for old coats and blankets for people in our city who don't have a warm place to sleep. Your answer was amazing. Here are the numbers: 412 coats, 156 blankets, 90 pairs of gloves and more than 200 warm hats. Everything was sorted by size and given to the Harbor Street Shelter, which helps hundreds of homeless people every winter.
>
> Here is how it worked. Most of the planning happened in the Maple Street Neighbors Chat, where people offered boxes, cars and free time. Harriet's café was our main collection point, and Harriet gave a free cookie to everyone who brought something. Tina Carter and Amy Brooks made posters and pinned them to every noticeboard on the street. Within two days, the boxes were full. On collection day, Trevor Carter and his friend Jake carried more than forty heavy boxes to Nathan Brooks's van, and they didn't complain once (well, maybe once).
>
> We also want to thank Mr. Alvarez, who has been deaf since birth. He taught our volunteers a few words in sign language, so they could say "hello" and "stay warm" to deaf visitors at the shelter. Several visitors stopped to talk with them, and one man laughed and corrected their hands.
>
> Volunteering does not have to take a lot of time. Some of you gave one hour, and some of you gave one coat, and both were contributions that mattered. Your kindness reached people who will never know your names.
>
> What's next? In February, we are putting together a plan for a little free library at the corner of Maple Street and Pine Street. If you would like to get involved, here is the person to talk to: Helena Brooks, at our next meeting on January 12.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 38) — Buổi đầu của câu lạc bộ tranh biện

> **Debate Club, First Meeting: Should Phones Be Banned in Class?**
>
> Ms. Park started a new debate club at Westview High in December. At the first meeting, two students practiced on a topic that everyone at school had an opinion about.
>
> **Lydia:** Maddie, you're first. Remember: give reasons, not just feelings.
>
> **Maddie:** Thank you. I firmly believe that phones should be banned during class. Although phones are useful, they are designed to grab our attention, and every message pulls us away from the lesson. There is also some evidence. A study in England found that after schools banned phones, students' test scores went up, especially for students who were struggling. Generally speaking, fewer distractions mean better learning.
>
> **Lydia:** Thank you, Maddie. Noah?
>
> **Noah:** I see your point, and I agree that phones can be a problem in class. But I don't think that a complete ban is the answer. For instance, many students use their phones to check the meaning of a word, take a photo of the board or use a calculator. Phones also help people work together outside school. Last month, our neighbors organized a whole coat drive through a group chat. Instead of banning phones, schools should teach students how to use them responsibly. Despite the risks, I think that the benefits outweigh the disadvantages.
>
> **Maddie:** With respect, schools have been trying that for years, and it hasn't worked. Teaching responsibility is great, but during a lesson, the phone should be in a bag.
>
> **Noah:** I suppose we could agree on that: phones away during the lesson, and out only when the teacher asks students to use them.
>
> **Lydia:** Interesting! You started on opposite sides and came to a partial agreement. That isn't a weakness in a debate. In real life, that's often where the best solutions are. Next week, you'll switch sides. Maddie will argue against the ban, and Noah will argue for it. Understanding the other viewpoint is the most useful skill that I can teach you.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `Here is / Here are` mở ra thông tin sắp nêu. Câu hỏi "What does … introduce?" chỉ cần đọc ngay phần sau dấu hai chấm.
- `although + mệnh đề` = `despite + danh từ` (dù). `because + mệnh đề` = `because of + danh từ` (vì).
- Bài tranh biện: ghi lại cho mỗi người một lý lẽ chính + một bằng chứng hoặc ví dụ. Câu hỏi NOT hay hỏi vào danh sách ví dụ.
- Phản bác lịch sự thường có hai bước: ghi nhận (`I see your point`, `I agree that…`) rồi mới nói `But…`.
