## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 39) — Ba ý tưởng quảng cáo của Chloe cho quán Harriet's

> **Harriet's Winter Campaign: Three Ideas**
>
> Prepared by: Chloe Bennett Design
>
> For: Harriet Brooks, Harriet's Café
>
> Harriet, you asked me for an advertising campaign to launch your new winter menu in January. Here are three ideas. Each one conveys a different message, so think about what you want people to remember.
>
> Idea A: "The Best Coffee in California"
>
> This is the boldest option. Short, strong slogans like this are memorable, and many cafés boast about their coffee in this way. However, it is a claim that nobody can prove, and some customers may find it hard to believe.
>
> Idea B: "Your Kitchen Away from Home"
>
> This idea puts the emphasis on the atmosphere rather than the food. The poster would show real regulars at their usual tables: Dustin with his Wednesday soup, the book club, and kids doing homework after school. (Of course, we would ask everyone first!) It's warm and honest, but it's less likely to tempt new customers who have never been inside.
>
> Idea C: "Warm Up at Harriet's"
>
> This one is visual: a large photo of a juicy apple pie and a cup of hot chocolate in vivid winter colors, plus a small offer: a free cookie with every drink during the first week of January. It's simple and tempting, and the offer gives people a reason to come in now. But it says little about what makes the café special.
>
> My recommendation: combine B and C. Use the photo and the offer to bring people in, and the real faces to show them why they should stay.
>
> Harriet's notes, written by hand at the bottom of the page:
>
> **Harriet:** No to A. I don't want to say anything about my café that I can't prove. My reputation is the only advertisement that I've ever needed. Yes to B and C together, but ask Dustin first. He's shy! And please add a small line saying that we sponsor the Maple Street Stars. I'm very proud of that.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 40) — Tracy giới thiệu công viên Balboa

> **A Local's Guide to Balboa Park**
>
> Written by: Tracy Carter, home in San Diego for winter break
>
> After two years in Washington, I've learned that most people there have heard of the San Diego Zoo, but few of them know that it sits inside one of the biggest city parks in the United States. Balboa Park covers about 1,200 acres, and it is so big that you could visit every weekend for a year and still find something new.
>
> Many of its most prominent buildings were built for a large international fair in 1915. Their white walls, colorful tiles and tall towers make the park look like a scene from an old movie. The California Tower, which is clearly visible from many parts of the city, is the best-known of them. Today, the park is home to more than fifteen museums, along with gardens, theaters and, of course, the zoo.
>
> My personal favorite is the Botanical Building, a giant wooden structure full of more than two thousand plants, with a long pond in front of it where you can catch a glimpse of colorful fish. On Sunday afternoons, you can sit outside and listen to a free concert on one of the biggest outdoor pipe organs in the world. If you would like somewhere quieter, the Japanese Friendship Garden has narrow paths, small waterfalls and wooden bridges.
>
> Here are a few tips from a local. Entry to the park itself is free, but most museums charge a fee, so check their websites before you go. Parking near the center fills up quickly on weekends, so arrive early or take the free tram from the parking lot at the edge of the park. And bring a jacket: even in San Diego, winter evenings are cooler than visitors expect.
>
> Walking through the park last week with my family, I realized how much I had missed home. Sometimes you have to go away before you can really see the place where you grew up.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Văn bản so sánh phương án: lập bảng nhỏ cho mỗi phương án (ưu điểm / nhược điểm). Câu hỏi hay hỏi điểm yếu, nằm sau chữ `However` hoặc `but`.
- Ghi chú phản hồi ở cuối tài liệu cho biết quyết định cuối cùng. Câu hỏi về kết quả phải đọc tới phần này.
- Bài giới thiệu địa điểm: tách số liệu (1,200 acres, 1915, fifteen museums). Phương án nhiễu thường đảo các con số này.
- Câu nối hai bài kiểu "nhân vật X có thể hưởng gì": kiểm thời gian và điều kiện ở cả hai bài.
