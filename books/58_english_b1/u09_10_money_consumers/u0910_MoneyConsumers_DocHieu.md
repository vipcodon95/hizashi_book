>

>
> The school soccer tryouts are in March, and my old boots are completely worn out. The boots that I want cost $120 in total, which is a lot of money for a fourteen-year-old. >
> However, I usually splash out on snacks after practice, and I often buy things for my phone games. So I made a list of my weekly expenses and decided to cut back on the things that are not worth it, for example, energy drinks and in-game coins.

I'm not sure whether I'm a good waiter or not, but I'm definitely a hungry one. Mom says that a real job will teach me more than any money lesson at school.
>
> By my calculations, I can afford the boots by the end of January, that is, six weeks before the tryouts. I bet Tina will still ask me for the extra dollar anyway.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 10) — Mua sắm online: biết quyền của mình

> **Shopping Online? Know Your Rights**
>
> Online shopping is so convenient that many of us now buy everything from shoes to washing powder without leaving the sofa. However, when a parcel arrives damaged, late or not at all, many shoppers do not know what to do. Here are a few things that every consumer should know.
>
> First, read the return policy before you pay. In the United States, there is no general law that says a store must accept returns of products that you simply do not like, so each company makes its own rules. Some offer free returns within thirty days, while others charge for delivery or give only store credit instead of a refund.
>
> Second, check the guarantee or warranty. A product that breaks quickly may be repaired or replaced, but only if you keep your receipt and order number. Third, watch out for hidden costs at the checkout, such as delivery fees, which can make a cheap item too expensive to buy.
>
> Fourth, a federal rule says that when a seller cannot send your order in the promised time, it must tell you and offer you the choice to cancel and get your money back. If the seller did not promise a date, the limit is usually thirty days. Finally, take photos of any damaged package before you open it, because they are useful evidence.
>
> Shopping around for a reasonable price is smart, but a great price is not such a good deal if you cannot send the product back.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Câu hỏi chủ đề nhật ký: đừng chọn một sự việc nhỏ (buổi tuyển chọn, việc dọn bàn). Hãy chọn điều mà mọi đoạn cùng nói tới (kế hoạch để dành tiền).
- `for example`, `such as`, `that is` báo hiệu ví dụ hoặc giải thích thêm. Đáp án câu hỏi chi tiết thường nằm ngay sau các từ này.
- Bài thông tin dạng liệt kê (`First… Second… Finally…`): đánh số từng lời khuyên bên lề để tìm nhanh.
- Câu áp dụng ("Một người mua giày… Ta có thể nói gì?"): tránh phương án tuyệt đối (`must`, `never`, `always`) khi bài chỉ nói "tuỳ từng nơi".
