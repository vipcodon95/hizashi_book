> **Luyện cho:** Unit 7 + Unit 8 khoá B1 · phiếu `b1.025` – `b1.032` (chọn nghề, công việc được giao, đơn xin việc, cân bằng công việc – cuộc sống; cuộc họp, hỏi lịch sự, xin phép trang trọng, email công việc).
>
> **Nhân vật:** Chloe Bennett, Harriet Brooks, Nathan Brooks, Laura Moore · giữa tháng 11 → đầu tháng 12, năm 4
>
> **Mục tiêu:** đọc một lá thư cá nhân và một email công việc; nắm mục đích, hiểu từ nối (however, therefore, meanwhile), câu hỏi gián tiếp và cách nói trang trọng (May I…?, Should you have any questions…), nhận ra giọng văn.

## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 7) — Lá thư của Chloe gửi bà Harriet

> **Dear Harriet**
>
> Dear Harriet,
>
> I'm writing this letter because I'm afraid that I will cry if I say it out loud. After all these wonderful years at the café, I have decided to leave at the end of December. It was the hardest decision of my life.
>
> As you know, I finished my evening graphic design class in the summer, and in September a small company asked me to design its new website. Since then, three more businesses have asked me to work for them. I'm sure that this is the right moment to start a career in design.
>
> However, I'm a little scared, because a freelance income is not as steady as a regular salary. Some months I will earn a lot, and other months almost nothing. My mom wants me to apply for a full-time job at a big firm instead, but I have already turned down two offers. Therefore, I will save money carefully and take on small jobs first.
>
> You were the first person who told me to follow my dream, and you even asked me to design the new sign for the café. Meanwhile, I don't want to leave you without help. My friend Ruby, who studies at the community college, is looking for part-time work, and she is very experienced in cafés. Shall I ask her to visit you next week?
>
> Of course, I will still come every Wednesday for soup with Dustin. Thank you for everything.
>
> Love, Chloe

>

Some companies have found that people concentrate better at home, while others say that teamwork suffers.
>
> Before we put forward a proposal, I would like to hear your ideas. Therefore, I have arranged a brainstorming meeting for Thursday, December 4, at 10 a.m. in Room 3B. The agenda is attached, but briefly, we will discuss which days we should work from home, how we will communicate, and how we will measure our progress. Nathan has kindly agreed to give a short presentation on the online tools that we already use. He will also explain how we can share documents safely.
>
> May I also ask you to complete the short survey in the second document before the meeting? I need to understand how much time each of you spends traveling to the office. Your answers will stay private, so please be honest.
>
> If you cannot come, please let me know by Tuesday, and I will ask someone to send you the notes. The meeting will take only one hour, so please do not call off any meetings with clients for it. Should you have any questions, please get back to me before Wednesday.
>
> Thanks, Laura Moore, Team Leader

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Câu hỏi mục đích thư: câu then chốt thường nằm ở đoạn đầu (`I have decided to…`, `I have arranged a meeting…`).
- Từ nối báo hiệu ý: `However` (trái ngược), `Therefore` (kết quả), `Meanwhile` (trong lúc đó). Câu hỏi suy luận hay xoay quanh các từ này.
- Email công việc: gạch chân ngày, giờ, phòng và các hạn chót khác nhau (`by Tuesday`, `before Wednesday`). Đề hay đánh tráo các mốc này.
- Câu hỏi giọng văn: tìm cách nói lịch sự (`May I…`, `kindly`, `Should you have…`) hay cảm xúc (`afraid`, `scared`).
- Câu nối hai bài: tìm điểm giống nhau về CÁCH viết (giải thích thay đổi + đưa kế hoạch), không tìm chi tiết giống nhau.
