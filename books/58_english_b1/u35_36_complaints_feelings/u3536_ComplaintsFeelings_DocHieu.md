# Units 35–36 — Problems and complaints & Feelings and stress (Phàn nàn, giải quyết vấn đề & Cảm xúc, áp lực)

> **Luyện cho:** Unit 35 + Unit 36 khoá B1 · phiếu `b1.137` – `b1.144` (as if / as though, viết khiếu nại, xin lỗi, giải quyết vấn đề; ước muốn với wish, căng thẳng và thư giãn, tâm trạng, an ủi bạn bè).
>
> **Nhân vật:** Harriet Brooks, Tracy Carter, Leah Carter · tháng 11–12, năm 5
>
> **Mục tiêu:** đọc một email khiếu nại kèm thư trả lời và một cuộc gọi điện thoại; hiểu `as if + quá khứ hoàn thành`, `explain / suggest to + người`, `wish + quá khứ / quá khứ hoàn thành`, cụm V-ing làm chủ ngữ, nhận ra giọng điệu và mục đích của từng phần thư.

## Từ vựng trước khi đọc

| Từ | Loại từ | Nghĩa | Phiếu |
|---|---|---|---|
| apology | danh từ | lời xin lỗi | `b1.139` |
| overwhelm | động từ | làm choáng ngợp | `b1.142` |
| stress | danh từ | căng thẳng, áp lực | `b1.142` |
| stressed | tính từ | bị căng thẳng | `b1.142` |
| counseling | danh từ | tư vấn tâm lý | `b1.144` |

**Từ ngoài bài học** (chưa có trong khoá tới unit này, xem nghĩa để đọc trôi):

| Từ | Nghĩa |
|---|---|
| sincere | chân thành (Yours sincerely = trân trọng) |
| inconvenience | sự phiền toái |
| delivery | lần giao hàng |
| supplier | nhà cung cấp |
| warehouse | nhà kho |
| refund | hoàn tiền |
| loyal | trung thành |
| finals | kỳ thi cuối kỳ |
| exhausted | kiệt sức |
| chest | ngực |

> 📌 Cụm hay gặp trong bài: *sort this out* (giải quyết việc này), *make up for* (bù đắp cho), *accept our apology* (nhận lời xin lỗi của chúng tôi), *fall behind* (tụt lại phía sau), *check on someone* (hỏi thăm ai), *take care of yourself* (chăm sóc bản thân).

## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 35) — Email khiếu nại của bà Harriet và thư trả lời

> **Subject: Problems with our last two deliveries**
>
> From: Harriet Brooks, Harriet's Café
>
> To: Customer Care, Coastline Coffee Company
>
> Dear Customer Care team,
>
> I have bought coffee from your company for almost ten years, and this is the first time that I have had to write to you like this. Unfortunately, the last two deliveries have had problems. On November 3 and November 17, the boxes arrived two days late, several bags were torn, and the beans inside were damp. Worse, the coffee tasted strange, as if it had been left in a hot car for a week. My regular customers noticed immediately. One gentleman, who has had the same coffee every morning for years, pushed his cup away and asked me if I had changed suppliers.
>
> I have explained to my staff that this is not their fault, but I cannot keep serving coffee like this. Could you please explain to me what has happened? I would also like to suggest a solution: please replace the damaged bags, and check the next delivery carefully before it leaves your warehouse.
>
> I hope that we can sort this out quickly. Yours sincerely, Harriet Brooks
>
> Subject: RE: Problems with our last two deliveries
>
> Dear Mrs. Brooks,
>
> Thank you for writing to us, and please accept our sincere apology. You are completely right. In early November, the cooling system in one of our delivery trucks broke down, and we did not notice it soon enough. Your boxes were in that truck. We have repaired the truck, and from now on, every box will be checked by two people before it leaves. To make up for the inconvenience, we will refund the full cost of both deliveries and send you two free bags of our new winter coffee. Loyal customers like you are the reason why we are still in business. If anything else goes wrong, please call me directly.
>
> Best regards, Rob Daniels, Customer Care Manager

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 36) — Cuộc gọi đêm khuya trước kỳ thi

> **A Late-Night Call Before Finals**
>
> It was 11:40 p.m. in Washington and 8:40 p.m. in San Diego when Tracy called her cousin.
>
> **Tracy:** Sorry, I know it's late. I just need to talk to someone. I have three exams next week, and I feel completely overwhelmed.
>
> **Leah:** Don't be sorry. I'm glad you called. What's going on?
>
> **Tracy:** I wish I had started studying earlier. I keep reading the same page again and again, and nothing stays in my head. And I haven't slept properly for days.
>
> **Leah:** OK. First, I'm not surprised that you're exhausted. When you're stressed and tired, your brain can't store new information well. Sleep isn't a waste of study time. It's part of studying.
>
> **Tracy:** But if I sleep, I'll fall even further behind.
>
> **Leah:** Try this. Make a plan for tomorrow with short blocks: forty-five minutes of studying, then a ten-minute break when you walk around or get some fresh air. Breaking a big job into small pieces makes it much less frightening. And when you feel your chest getting tight, try slow breathing: breathe in for four seconds, hold for four, and breathe out for four. It sounds too simple, but it really does calm your body.
>
> **Tracy:** Camila said the same thing. She's been bringing me tea and making me laugh, but I still feel as if I'm the only one who doesn't understand anything.
>
> **Leah:** I can promise you that you're not. Half of your class probably feels exactly the same. I felt like that before every exam in medical school. One more thing: talk to people. Your school has a counseling center, and it's free for students. Going there doesn't mean that you're weak. It means that you're taking care of yourself.
>
> **Tracy:** Thanks, Leah. I wish you were here.
>
> **Leah:** Me too. But I'll call you tomorrow night to check on you. Now close the book and go to bed. That's a doctor's order!
>
> Tracy slept for eight hours that night. On Friday, she sent a message to the family chat: "Two exams done. I think they went OK!"

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Thư khiếu nại tốt có 3 phần: nêu sự việc (ngày, chi tiết) → hậu quả → đề xuất. Câu hỏi mục đích hay hỏi đúng cấu trúc này.
- `as if + quá khứ hoàn thành` là so sánh tưởng tượng, không phải sự thật. Loại phương án hiểu theo nghĩa đen.
- `I wish I had + V3` = tiếc về quá khứ; `I wish you were here` = ước điều không có thật ở hiện tại.
- Câu hỏi giọng điệu: tìm các cụm như `please accept our sincere apology`, `You are completely right`.
