# Unit 4 — Home life (Cuộc sống gia đình)

> **Luyện cho:** Unit 4 khoá B1 · phiếu `b1.013` – `b1.016` (sửa chữa trong nhà, việc nhà và chia việc, cải tạo nhà cửa, sống chung với người khác).
>
> **Nhân vật:** Megan Carter, Tracy Carter, Trevor Carter, Mitchell Carter, Leah Carter, Tina Carter, Biscuit, Harriet Brooks, Nathan Brooks, Helena Brooks, Amy Brooks · giữa tháng 10, năm 4 (bài 1: email Megan viết thứ Tư 15/10 cho Tracy ở Washington; ống nước rò tối thứ Hai 13/10, thợ tới sáng thứ Ba 14/10; lịch năm 4 theo mẫu năm 2025; Trevor 14 tuổi đang dùng phòng cũ của Tracy; bài 2: bài báo về gia đình nhiều thế hệ, lấy nhà Brooks ở số 15 làm ví dụ)
>
> **Mục tiêu:** đọc email Megan kể cho Tracy chuyện ống nước bị rò ở nhà Carter, và bài báo về gia đình nhiều thế hệ sống chung một nhà; hiểu cấu trúc have / get something done (we had the whole thing looked over, we're getting the carpet cleaned, had a handrail put in), câu mệnh lệnh + and / or + kết quả, câu hỏi đáp lại (Is it? Would you?), hoà hợp chủ ngữ với as well as, cụm động từ look over, put in, put up with; tìm chi tiết, nắm ý chính và mục đích, đoán nghĩa từ, hiểu đại từ chỉ gì, câu NOT, thái độ người viết và 4 câu suy luận.

## Bài đọc 1 — Email của Megan kể cho Tracy vụ vỡ ống nước

> **Subject: The Great Bathroom Flood**
>
> **Wednesday, October 15**
>
> **Hi Tracy,**
>
> You wanted news, so here it is: on Monday night, a pipe under the upstairs bathroom sink started leaking. By the time Trevor noticed, the carpet in his room (your old room) was wet. When I told him, "The pipe is leaking," he said, "Is it? I thought Biscuit knocked over his water bowl again!"
>
> Your dad tried to tighten the pipe himself, but that only made it worse. So on Tuesday morning, we had the whole thing looked over by a plumber named Vince Ortiz. Leah was asleep downstairs after a night shift, so I told Vince, "Don't bang anything, or you'll wake a very tired doctor." He was kind and even whispered, but there were still a few loud thumps when he moved the cupboard.
>
> Biscuit, of course, made everything harder: he stole one of Vince's gloves and hid it under Trevor's bed. Vince found it next to three of Trevor's socks and laughed, "Every house has a helper like this."
>
> The old pipe was bent, so he took it out and put in a new one. He also checked the other pipes and gave us some advice: "Have them looked at once a year and you'll save money." Now we're getting the carpet cleaned, and next month we're having the roof checked before the winter rain.

> Trevor told me, "I'd love a new carpet with a football field on it!" I said, "Would you? Keep your room tidy for a month and I'll think about it." Call on Sunday, and Tina will tell you the whole story again, with more drama.
>
> Love, Mom

## Bài đọc 2 — Bài báo về gia đình nhiều thế hệ sống chung một nhà

> **Three Generations, One Roof**
>
> In the United States, more and more grandparents, parents and children share one home: the Pew Research Center found about 18 percent of Americans lived in a home with several generations in 2021, four times as many people as in 1971. Money is the most common reason, but caring for an older parent or a young child also matters.
>
> **Why it works**
>
> At 15 Maple Street, Harriet Brooks, 71, lives with her son Nathan, his wife Helena and their seven-year-old daughter Amy. Harriet still runs her café, and in the evenings she reads to Amy while Nathan and Helena finish work. "People think I live with my son's family," she laughs. "Actually, they live with me!" Families like the Brooks share bills, cooking and housework, and children grow up close to their grandparents. In a Pew survey, more than half of adults in these homes said living together was usually convenient.
>
> **What can go wrong**
>
> Still, about a quarter said it was usually stressful. One person wants loud music while another wants to sleep, storage space disappears, and a small mess can turn into a big argument. "We all had different ideas about an orderly kitchen," Helena admits.
>
> **Tips for sharing a home**
>
> Talk about money early, and decide together who pays for what. Share the housework evenly, or one person will end up doing everything. Change the house to fit the people in it: the Brooks family had a handrail put in on the stairs, and Amy, as well as her grandmother, uses it every day. As Nathan says, "You learn to put up with small things, and you get a lot back."

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- **have / get + vật + V3**: người nói không tự làm mà nhờ hoặc thuê người khác. `we had the whole thing looked over by a plumber` = thợ kiểm tra, không phải nhà Carter. Khi câu hỏi hỏi ai làm việc gì, đừng nhầm người nhờ với người làm.
- **Câu mệnh lệnh + and / or**: `… and you'll save a lot of money` = làm thì được lợi; `… or you'll wake up a very tired doctor` = không làm thì gặp hậu quả. Phần sau and / or thường là đáp án cho câu hỏi Why hoặc câu hỏi về lời khuyên.
- **Câu hỏi đáp lại** (Is it? Would you? Did he?) chỉ để tỏ ra đang nghe, không phải câu hỏi thật. Ý thật của người nói nằm ở câu ngay sau đó.
- **A, as well as B, + động từ số ít**: `Amy, as well as her grandmother, uses it` — động từ theo A, nhưng về nghĩa cả hai người cùng làm.
- Bài báo có nhiều con số (18 percent, more than half, a quarter): ghi chú mỗi số đi với thông tin gì. Phương án nhiễu hay ghép đúng số với sai thông tin.
