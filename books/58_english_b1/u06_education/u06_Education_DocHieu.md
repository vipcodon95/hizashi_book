## Bài đọc 1 — Email của Tracy từ Washington

> **Subject: Sophomore Life**
>
> Hi there,
>
> Greetings from Washington, where the leaves are already red and orange! My roommate and I have already bought warm boots for the first snow. I'm a sophomore now, and I can hardly believe that my second academic year has already started.
>
> At first, the engineering curriculum felt overwhelming. Some of my lectures have three hundred students, and the professors speak so fast that it is hard for me to keep up with them. >
> The hardest part is the exams. Before my first examination, my anxiety was so bad that I barely slept. Now I revise a little every day instead of studying all night, and it really helps. I also attend a study group every Thursday, and we explain difficult problems to one another.
>
> How is Trevor's math? Tina says that you are a much stricter teacher than I was! I'll be home in December, just in time for Harriet's winter cookies.
>

## Bài đọc 2 — Học trực tuyến hay học trực tiếp?

> **Online or in Person? Choosing How to Learn**
>
> Ten years ago, most students learned in a classroom. Today, many schools and universities offer online courses, and students can sign in from their kitchen, a café or a bus. So which way of learning is better? The honest answer is that it depends on the student.
>
> Online learning has clear advantages. It is accessible to people who live far from a school or who work during the day, and recorded lectures can be watched again and again. Students can also learn at their own pace, which suits people who need more time for difficult subjects. Online courses are often cheaper, too, because students do not have to pay for travel or housing.
>
> However, learning at home needs a lot of motivation. Without a teacher in the room, it is easy to lose concentration and to check messages instead. Some students also feel lonely, because a webcam cannot fully replace a conversation in the canteen. A good internet connection is also necessary, and not every family has one.
>
> Classroom learning, on the other hand, gives students a clear routine and quick help from teachers and classmates. Questions can be answered immediately, and group work is usually easier when everyone is in the same room. For this reason, many experts now suggest a mix of the two: lessons in person, with online materials for revision.
>
> In my opinion, the question is not "online or in person?" but "what helps you to learn best?" Before you register for a course, think about your habits honestly, and choose the one that fits your life.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Câu hỏi mục đích email: đọc câu chào và câu kết, rồi gom các đoạn giữa xem người viết đang kể, nhờ, hay than phiền.
- `in time` = kịp (trước khi việc gì xảy ra); `on time` = đúng giờ đã hẹn. `at first` = lúc đầu (sau đó thay đổi); `in the end` = cuối cùng.
- Bài nêu quan điểm thường có cấu trúc: ưu điểm → nhược điểm (`However…`) → ý kiến (`In my opinion…`). Câu hỏi thái độ nằm ở đoạn ý kiến.
- Câu hỏi "học sinh nào sẽ thấy khó nhất": tìm trong đoạn NHƯỢC ĐIỂM, vì ba phương án còn lại thường lấy từ đoạn ưu điểm.
