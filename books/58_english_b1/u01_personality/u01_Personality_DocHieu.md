## Bài đọc 1 — Bài luận của Tina: người em ngưỡng mộ

> **A Person I Admire**
>
> On my first day at Westview Middle School, I was quite nervous because I didn't know anyone. When I got home, Harriet Brooks was waiting outside with hot chocolate and said, "Tell me everything!"
>
> She is the neighbor who lives across the street, and at seventy-one, she is more cheerful and hard-working than anyone I know. She taught second grade for almost forty years and then opened Harriet's, a café which is now the heart of Maple Street. She is pretty talkative and knows everybody's news, but she is also honest, so people trust her with their problems. When she turned seventy, her family planned a surprise party, and keeping it secret from her was hard!
>
> On Saturday mornings, she helps kids with homework at the café, and her patience is remarkable. Once I got a math problem wrong five times, but she never lost her temper. She believes curious people ask the best questions, so she never gets tired of my "Why?" My dad learned to make her vegetable soup, and he loves her salt rule: "You can add more later, but you can never take it out."
>
> She has weaknesses, too: she is a bit stubborn about technology, so next to the café's tablet she still keeps her old wooden money box "just in case."
>
> Her hands are getting slower, but her laugh is getting louder. Middle school is getting easier, too, because I remember her advice: be brave, be kind, and ask questions. I really look up to her, and I hope I resemble her a little when I am old.

## Bài đọc 2 — Hướng nội, hướng ngoại và tất cả những người ở giữa

> **Introverts, Extroverts, and Everyone in Between**
>
> Do you love big parties, or do you prefer a quiet evening with one friend? Psychologists use two words for this difference: introvert and extrovert. Carl Jung, a Swiss doctor who studied the human mind, made these words famous in a 1921 book.
>
> **Where does your energy come from?**
>
> Extroverts get energy from other people and can feel bored when they are alone for too long. Introverts can enjoy a party, too, but afterwards they usually need quiet time to rest. Many people think introverts are always shy, but shyness is a fear of what others think of you, and that is different. An introvert can be confident in class but still prefer lunch with one friend.
>
> **A line, not two boxes**
>
> Today, most psychologists see personality as a spectrum: a long line with very quiet people at one end and very social people at the other. Most of us are somewhere in the middle. Your place on the line can differ, too: someone talkative at home may be silent at a new school.
>
> **One word is not enough**
>
> Online personality quizzes can be fun, but a single word can't characterize a whole person. A teacher who calls a child "the quiet one" may stop that child from trying new things. Your family, friends, and experiences all shape you, so no group of people is simply quiet or loud. Similarly, neither type is better: the world needs people who listen and people who speak up. So you are never just an introvert: you are also curious, kind, funny, and more.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- **who / which** chỉ danh từ đứng ngay trước nó: `the neighbor who lives across the street` (who = người), `a café which is now the heart of Maple Street` (which = vật). Câu hỏi quy chiếu hay đặt phương án nhiễu là một danh từ khác trong cùng câu.
- **Cụm động từ** (look up to = kính trọng) mang nghĩa mới, không đoán được từ từng chữ. **Động từ + giới từ** (get energy **from**, think **of**) vẫn giữ nghĩa gốc. Gặp cụm lạ, hãy đọc câu trước và chữ but / so để đoán nghĩa.
- **be getting + so sánh hơn** (getting slower, getting easier) = ngày càng… Câu hỏi "what is changing…?" lấy đáp án từ cấu trúc này.
- **quite / pretty / a bit** làm nhẹ tính từ: `pretty talkative` = khá nói nhiều, `a bit stubborn` = hơi cứng đầu. Phương án nhiễu hay phóng đại (too talkative, very stubborn) để đánh lừa.
- Câu suy luận và câu áp dụng: bài không nói thẳng. Ghép hai câu đứng cạnh nhau, hoặc đặt từng phương án cạnh định nghĩa trong bài, rồi loại phương án làm NGƯỢC hay mô tả một khái niệm khác (shy ≠ introvert).
