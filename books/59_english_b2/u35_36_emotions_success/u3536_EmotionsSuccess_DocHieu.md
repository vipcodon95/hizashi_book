## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 35) — Nhật ký của Leah: cuộc cãi nhau và làm lành

> **Leah's Journal: The Offer**
>
> Monday night
>
> Ethan told me tonight, over dinner, that he has been offered a place on a special wildfire training program in the north of the state. It would last six months. He was absolutely thrilled, and I should have been happy for him. Instead, I was furious. It wasn't the program. It was the fact that he had known about it for two weeks and had already said yes. Were I in his position, I would have asked my partner first. I told him that, not very calmly, and he went quiet in the way that I hate.
>
> Wednesday
>
> Two days of short, polite messages. I am absolutely exhausted, and not only because of work. I talked to Chloe, who listened for an hour and then asked one question: "Are you angry that he's going, or that he didn't ask you?" It was the second one, of course. Were it not for Chloe, I might well have spent another week being angry about the wrong thing.
>
> Friday
>
> Tonight was our Thursday-evening rule, moved to Friday because of my shift. We almost cancelled it. I am very glad we didn't. Ethan apologized first. He said that he had been afraid that I would ask him not to go, and that he wouldn't be able to say no to me. That made me cry, which made him panic, which made us both laugh. Then I told him the truth: I want him to go. Six months is a long time, but it is not forever, and it is the kind of training that he has dreamed about since I met him. We made a plan: a video call every night that neither of us is working, and I will visit once a month.
>
> I keep thinking that love, like anything worth doing, may well need practice. We are getting better at it.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 36) — Sự thật về "quy tắc 10.000 giờ"

> **The Truth About the 10,000-Hour Rule**
>
> If you have ever wanted to become an expert at something, you may well have heard of the "10,000-hour rule": the idea that anyone can become world-class at anything after ten thousand hours of practice. It is a hopeful idea. Unfortunately, it is not quite what the research says.
>
> The number comes from a study published in 1993 by the psychologist Anders Ericsson and his colleagues, who studied violin students at a music academy in Berlin. They found that, by the age of twenty, the best students had practiced for about ten thousand hours on average, much more than the less successful students. The idea became famous after a best-selling book in 2008 turned it into a simple "rule".
>
> Ericsson himself later said that the rule had been misunderstood. First, ten thousand was an average: some of the best violinists had practiced far less, and some far more. Second, and more importantly, not all practice is equal. What made the difference was "deliberate practice": focused work on specific weaknesses, usually with a teacher and with clear feedback, which is often difficult and not much fun. Playing the songs that you already know for ten thousand hours could well make you very comfortable, but it will not necessarily make you better.
>
> Later research has added another surprise. A large review in 2014 combined dozens of studies and found that practice explained only part of the differences between people: about a fifth in music and sports, and much less in many jobs. Other things, such as the age when people start, their personality and simple luck, also matter.
>
> None of this means that practice is pointless. Nobody becomes excellent without a great deal of it. But the real lesson may be less about counting hours and more about how those hours are spent, and about being kind to yourself when progress is slower than a famous number promised.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `Were I…, I would…` = `If I were…`; `Were it not for X` = nếu không có X. Đảo ngữ điều kiện hay xuất hiện trong văn viết, nhật ký, bài luận.
- Tính từ tuyệt đối (`exhausted`, `furious`, `thrilled`) đi với `absolutely`, không đi với `very`. Chúng cho biết cảm xúc rất mạnh.
- `may well / might well / could well` = rất có thể. Mạnh hơn `may` một chút.
- Bài phản bác một quan niệm: tìm câu "it is not quite what the research says" rồi theo dõi từng ý First, Second, Later research.
