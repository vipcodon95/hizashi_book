## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 15) — Helena biến bãi đỗ xe thành công viên nhỏ

> **From Parking Lot to Pocket Park: How It Will Happen**
>
> Written by: Helena Brooks, for the Maple Street Neighbors Group
>
> Behind the Pine Street Library, there is a parking lot with forty spaces. It is about the size of six tennis courts, and on most days, fewer than ten cars are parked there. The fact that it is almost always empty, while our street has no park within walking distance, is what gave us the idea. After two neighborhood meetings and a survey that 214 of you answered, the library has agreed to turn the lot into a small public park.
>
> Here is how the work will be done. First, in October, the old fence will be taken down and the asphalt will be removed. About a third of it will be broken up and used again under the new paths. Next, the ground will be prepared, and a small drain will be added in the lowest corner so that rainwater doesn't collect there. Once the ground is ready, twelve native trees will be planted along the north side, where they will give shade to the benches in the afternoon. Finally, a gentle ramp will be built at the entrance next to the library, so that the park can be reached easily by people in wheelchairs and parents with strollers.
>
> In the southeast corner, opposite the ramp, you will find the little free library that the neighbors group has been planning since last winter. Ten parking spaces will be kept along the street for library visitors, which should be more than enough, judging from the survey.
>
> Some neighbors have asked whether removing parking will cause problems. We have taken this into account. During the busy library events on Saturday mornings, cars will be allowed to use the community center's parking lot across the road, by agreement with its owners. If all goes to plan, the park will be open by the end of January. Volunteers for the tree-planting weekend are very welcome. Bring gloves!

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 16) — Rừng tảo bẹ California biến mất và hồi sinh

> **The Disappearing Forests Under the Sea**
>
> Off the coast of California, there are forests that most people will never see. They are made of kelp, a type of large seaweed that can grow up to half a meter in a single day, and they are home to fish, seals, sea otters and thousands of smaller creatures.
>
> Since 2014, however, more than 90 percent of the kelp forests along parts of the northern California coast have disappeared. Scientists believe that several problems arrived at almost the same time. First, an unusually long period of warm water, which lasted from 2014 to 2016, weakened the kelp, which grows best in cold water. At around the same time, a disease killed millions of sea stars along the coast, including the sunflower sea star, one of the main predators of purple sea urchins. With nothing to control them, the urchins multiplied rapidly and ate their way through what was left of the kelp.
>
> Where forests once stood, there are now "urchin barrens": rocky sea floors covered in thousands of urchins and almost nothing else. The damage has spread to people, too. Had the kelp survived, many divers and fishing businesses would not have lost their work. If the sea stars had not died, scientists say, the urchins would probably have been kept under control.
>
> There is some hope. In several small areas, volunteer divers have removed urchins by hand, and in some of those places, kelp has started to grow back within a year or two. Further south, around Monterey, sea otters help, because they eat large numbers of urchins. Scientists are also trying to breed sunflower sea stars in laboratories so that they can one day be returned to the wild.
>
> Kelp forests can recover quickly, but only if the balance between plants and animals is restored. As one diver put it, "You can't just plant a forest. You have to give it a reason to stay."

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Văn bản quy trình: đánh dấu `First`, `Next`, `Once…`, `Finally`. Câu hỏi thứ tự và mục đích (`so that…`) nằm trong từng bước.
- Ngôn ngữ vị trí (`in the southeast corner, opposite the ramp`): vẽ nhanh một sơ đồ nhỏ ra giấy nháp.
- `Had + S + V3, … would have + V3` = If + S + had V3: chuyện trái với quá khứ. Đáp án đúng luôn nói điều NGƯỢC LẠI đã xảy ra.
- Chuỗi nguyên nhân (nước ấm → sao biển chết → nhím tăng → tảo bẹ mất): câu hỏi "Why…?" thường hỏi đúng mắt xích ngay trước.
