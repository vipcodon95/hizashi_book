## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 27) — Megan học tiếng Tây Ban Nha cho bệnh nhân

> **"I Didn't Dare to Speak": A Nurse Learns Spanish**
>
> Sunset Bay Hospital staff newsletter · People of the Month
>
> Megan Carter has worked as an emergency nurse at Sunset Bay for almost twenty years. Last year, she decided to learn Spanish, and this month we asked her why. "In San Diego, a lot of our patients speak Spanish at home, and some of them speak very little English," she explains. "We have excellent interpreters, but they can't be in every room at three in the morning." One night changed her mind. An elderly woman with chest pain was too frightened to answer questions, and her ten-year-old grandson had to translate. "Hospitals try hard not to use children as interpreters, and for good reason," Megan says. "He was terrified, and I couldn't even tell his grandmother that she was going to be OK."
>
> She started with an app and an evening class at the community college. The beginning was humbling. "For months, I didn't dare to speak to real patients. I was afraid of saying something wrong at the worst possible moment." Her colleague Marisol, who grew up speaking Spanish, finally lost patience. "She told me, 'How dare you be so perfect in English and so shy in Spanish? Just try!'"
>
> So she tried. Her first sentence to a patient was, she admits, grammatically terrible. The patient smiled and answered slowly. "That's when I realized that people don't need me to be perfect. They need me to try." Megan is clear that a few words do not replace a trained interpreter, especially for serious medical decisions. "I would never dare to explain a surgery in my Spanish," she laughs.
>
> But she can now say "You are safe", "Your family is here" and "Does it hurt more now?", and she says that these small sentences have changed how patients look at her. "Their faces relax. For a moment, the hospital is a little less foreign."

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 28) — Một xã hội đang già đi

> **Growing Older Together: America's Changing Age**
>
> There have been many changes in American society over the last fifty years, but one of the biggest is also one of the quietest: the country is getting older. In 2022, more than 57 million Americans, about one in six, were aged 65 or older, and the number is growing every year. Experts expect that, within the next decade or so, older adults will outnumber children for the first time in the nation's history.
>
> There are two main reasons. People are living longer, thanks to better medicine and healthier lives, and families are having fewer children than in the past. The large generation born after 1945, often called the baby boomers, has also now reached retirement age.
>
> This change brings challenges. There have already been shortages of nurses and home care workers in many areas, and the cost of caring for the elderly is rising. Many adults in their fifties now find themselves caring for their parents and their teenage children at the same time, with little time left for themselves. And as earlier research has shown, loneliness is a serious health risk, especially for people who live alone.
>
> But an older society also has strengths that are often forgotten. Older people volunteer, care for grandchildren, start businesses and pass down skills that would otherwise be lost. Some communities have started programs that bring generations together: in one well-known example in Seattle, a preschool has been running inside a home for older people since the early 1990s, and the children and residents paint, sing and eat together every week.
>
> The question, then, is not whether America will grow older; it already is. The question is whether communities will be designed so that the old and the young keep meeting each other, or whether they will live in separate worlds.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `not dare (to) + V` = không dám. `How dare you…!` thường là giận, nhưng giữa bạn bè có thể là đùa. Xem câu sau để biết.
- `the + tính từ` (the elderly, the young) = cả nhóm người, động từ chia số nhiều.
- Bài phỏng vấn: lời nhân vật nằm trong ngoặc kép. Câu hỏi về quan điểm phải lấy đúng lời của người đó.
- Câu "The question is not X; … The question is Y" cho biết người viết nghĩ điều gì mới quan trọng: Y.
