## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 39) — Ethan hướng dẫn chuẩn bị "túi đi ngay" mùa cháy rừng

> **Before the Smoke Arrives: A Firefighter's Guide to Your Go-Bag**
>
> Written by: Ethan Hayes, for the Maple Street Neighborhood News
>
> I am writing this from a training camp in the north of the state, where I have spent the last two months learning to fight wildfires in the mountains. If there is one thing that I have learned here, it is this: the people who are ready before a fire are the people who leave safely. In Southern California, wildfire season now lasts much longer than it used to, and you do not have to live in a forest to be at risk. Smoke and sparks can travel for miles.
>
> Step one: pack a go-bag.
>
> A go-bag is a bag that is packed and waiting near your door, so that you have nothing to look for if you need to leave in ten minutes. Put in water, at least one gallon per person per day for three days, any medicines that you take, copies of important documents, a phone charger, a flashlight, some cash and a few masks that can filter smoke. If you have pets, don't forget food and a leash. Biscuit, my neighbor's dog, would want me to mention that.
>
> Step two: know two ways out.
>
> Roads can be closed by fire or blocked by traffic, so plan at least two routes out of the neighborhood, and agree with your family on a place to meet if you are separated.
>
> Step three: be ready to go early.
>
> When officials say that it is time to leave, leave. Don't be the person who waits to see how bad it gets. Many of the most dangerous situations that I have seen in training began with someone who thought that there was nothing to worry about yet. Packing a go-bag takes about an hour. It is the kind of job that is easy to put off. Please don't. Be prepared, be calm, and look after your neighbors, especially those who might need help to leave.

- B. so that you can visit friends in other neighborhoods before you leave *(để bạn ghé thăm bạn bè ở các khu khác trước khi rời đi)*
- C. because the city requires every family to plan two routes in writing *(vì thành phố bắt mỗi gia đình phải lập hai tuyến bằng văn bản)*
- D. because roads can be closed by fire or blocked by traffic when people leave *(vì đường có thể bị lửa chặn hoặc kẹt xe khi mọi người cùng rời đi)*

**8. What does "Don't be the person who waits to see how bad it gets" mean?**
*(Câu "Đừng là người cứ chờ xem tình hình tệ tới mức nào" nghĩa là gì?)*

- A. Wait until you can see the fire from your window, and then decide whether to leave. *(Chờ tới khi thấy lửa từ cửa sổ, rồi mới quyết định có đi không.)*
- B. Leave as soon as officials tell you to, instead of waiting to see what happens. *(Hãy rời đi ngay khi các viên chức báo, thay vì chờ xem chuyện gì xảy ra.)*
- C. Stay at home and watch the news until officials call you on the phone. *(Ở nhà xem tin tức cho tới khi các viên chức gọi điện cho bạn.)*
- D. Call a firefighter first and ask whether the situation is really bad. *(Gọi cho lính cứu hoả trước và hỏi xem tình hình có thật sự tệ không.)*

**9. According to Ethan, how did many of the most dangerous situations begin?**
*(Theo Ethan, nhiều tình huống nguy hiểm nhất bắt đầu thế nào?)*

- A. with someone who thought that there was nothing to worry about yet *(từ một người nghĩ rằng chưa có gì phải lo)*
- B. with bad weather that nobody had expected, such as strong wind or heavy rain *(từ thời tiết xấu không ai ngờ tới, như gió mạnh hay mưa to)*
- C. with a broken phone, so that people could not hear the call to leave *(từ một chiếc điện thoại hỏng, nên người ta không nghe được lời kêu gọi rời đi)*
- D. with too many firefighters arriving at the same time on a narrow road *(từ quá nhiều lính cứu hoả cùng đến một lúc trên con đường hẹp)*

**10. What does Ethan's last paragraph suggest about why people don't prepare?**
*(Đoạn cuối của Ethan cho thấy vì sao người ta không chuẩn bị?)*

- A. Go-bags are very expensive, so most families can't pay for one. *(Túi đi ngay rất đắt, nên hầu hết các gia đình không mua nổi.)*
- B. Nobody knows how to pack one, because firefighters never explain it. *(Không ai biết cách xếp, vì lính cứu hoả chưa bao giờ giải thích.)*
- C. It is an easy job to put off until later, so people keep waiting. *(Đó là việc dễ trì hoãn, nên người ta cứ chờ mãi.)*
- D. They don't believe firefighters, who often tell them to leave too early. *(Họ không tin lính cứu hoả, những người hay bảo họ đi quá sớm.)*

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 40) — Bà Harriet và cửa hàng trống bên cạnh

> **The Empty Shop Next Door**
>
> The bakery next to Harriet's café had been closed for three months when the sign appeared in the window: FOR RENT. Harriet stood in front of it for a long time. That evening, she invited Nathan for dinner, which he knew was never only about dinner.
>
> **Nathan:** You must be thinking about the shop next door.
>
> **Harriet:** I might be. If I took it, I could double the number of tables. I could start breakfast earlier, and the book club wouldn't have to sit on the stairs.
>
> **Nathan:** Mom, you're seventy-three. You already work six days a week.
>
> **Harriet:** I know how old I am, thank you. That's exactly why I'm thinking about it now and not in ten years.
>
> **Nathan:** What does the money look like?
>
> **Harriet:** A friend of Ruby's, the one who studies business, has been helping me with the numbers. The rent is high, but the café is busier than it has ever been. Most days, people are waiting for a table by nine.
>
> **Nathan:** So what's stopping you?
>
> Harriet was quiet for a moment.
>
> **Harriet:** Change. People don't come here for a big café. They come because it feels like a kitchen. What if I make it bigger and it stops feeling like home? I might be ruining the one thing that works.
>
> **Nathan:** Then don't make it bigger. Make it different. Use the new room for the book club, the kids' story mornings and the neighbors' dinners. Keep the old room exactly as it is.
>
> Harriet looked at her son as if he had just said something very clever, which, for once, he had. "I'm not deciding tonight," she said. "But I'm going to call about the rent tomorrow. Just to ask." Nathan smiled. In his experience, his mother never called "just to ask".

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Bài hướng dẫn có các bước (`Step one`, `Step two`…). Câu hỏi NOT hay hỏi vào danh sách đồ dùng trong một bước.
- `must be + V-ing` = hẳn là đang; `might be + V-ing` = có thể đang. Đây là suy đoán về hiện tại, không phải nghĩa vụ.
- Hội thoại gia đình: câu đáp cộc (`I know how old I am, thank you`) cho thấy nhân vật hơi phật ý. Đọc câu sau để biết lý do.
- Kết thúc mở: câu cuối thường gợi ý điều sẽ xảy ra (`never called just to ask`). Câu hỏi suy luận hỏi đúng chỗ này.
