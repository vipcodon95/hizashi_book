## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 37) — Biên bản họp: chuyện xe chạy nhanh trên phố

> **Meeting Notes: Slowing Down Maple Street**
>
> Maple Street Neighbors Group · Monthly meeting at the Pine Street Library · Notes by Helena Brooks
>
> The problem
>
> Since the pocket park opened in January, many more children have been crossing Maple Street to reach it, and several neighbors have reported cars driving dangerously fast. Last month, Trevor Carter and two friends counted cars for a week with a borrowed speed gun. Of the 640 cars that they measured, about 60 percent were going faster than the 25-mile-per-hour limit. Most were not local drivers but people using Maple Street as a shortcut in order not to wait at the traffic lights on Ocean Avenue.
>
> Ideas discussed
>
> Speed bumps were the most popular idea, but they have to be approved by the city's traffic department, which can take months. In the meantime, several faster solutions were suggested. Volunteers will stand at the crossing near the park between 3 and 5 p.m. on school days, wearing bright vests, so as not to leave children crossing alone at the busiest time. Tina Carter and Amy Brooks have offered to organize children to paint signs that ask drivers to slow down. Nathan Brooks suggested asking map apps to stop sending drivers down Maple Street as a shortcut. Several people laughed, but he pointed out that some cities have done this successfully.
>
> One disagreement
>
> Mr. Hollis's cousin, who lives at the end of the street, warned the group not to make the street "unfriendly to drivers", since he drives to work every day. After some discussion, everyone agreed that the goal is not to stop people driving, but to make them drive at a safe speed.
>
> Decisions
>
> Helena will send the speed data to the city with a request for two speed bumps. Volunteers for the crossing should sign up by Friday. The children's signs will go up next weekend.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 38) — Có nên dùng AI viết bài luận xin vào đại học?

> **Should AI Write Your College Essay?**
>
> As any student who has applied to college knows, the personal essay is often the hardest part of the application. You have about 650 words to show a stranger who you are. It is no surprise, then, that many students are tempted to ask an AI tool to do it for them.
>
> Supporters of using AI argue that it simply levels the playing field. Wealthy families have always paid private advisers to help their children write and rewrite their essays. Why, they ask, should a free tool be treated differently? Used carefully, AI can help students who have no one at home to give them feedback, for example by pointing out unclear sentences or suggesting questions to think about.
>
> Critics see an important difference between help and replacement. An essay is meant to show how a student thinks and what they have experienced. If a machine writes it, the reader learns nothing true about the applicant. As is often the case with new technology, the rules are still being written, but many colleges have made it clear that submitting writing produced by AI as your own can be treated as dishonest. And as readers at colleges have pointed out, AI essays tend to sound oddly similar: polished, general and strangely empty.
>
> Trevor Carter, a senior at Westview High who applied to study environmental engineering this year, wrote all three drafts of his essay himself. "I asked an AI tool once to tell me which parts were confusing, and that was useful," he said. "But when I read the version that it wrote, it didn't sound like me at all. If a computer had written it, it wouldn't have been my story."
>
> Perhaps that is the clearest test. Use any tool that helps you think more clearly, but ask yourself, as you would with a parent or a teacher: are these still my ideas and my words? If the answer is no, the essay may be better written, but it will not be about you.

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- `in order not to / so as not to + V` = để không… Câu hỏi "Why…?" hay có đáp án nằm ngay sau cụm này.
- Biên bản họp: mục `Decisions` cho biết ai sẽ làm gì, khi nào. Câu hỏi về kết quả luôn lấy ở đây.
- `as` đầu câu có thể là "như" (As any student knows…) hoặc "vì" (As he drives to work…). Đọc vế sau để biết.
- Bài đạo đức: người viết thường kết bằng một phép thử (`ask yourself…`). Câu hỏi quan điểm của người viết lấy ở đó.
