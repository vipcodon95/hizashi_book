# Units 43–44 — Opinions and reviews & Presentations (Ý kiến, nhận xét & Thuyết trình)

> **Luyện cho:** Unit 43 + Unit 44 khoá B2 · phiếu `b2.169` – `b2.176` (ý kiến, nhận xét, đánh giá; thuyết trình, phỏng vấn, mở rộng câu trả lời khi nói).
>
> **Nhân vật:** Trevor Carter, Tracy Carter, Leah Carter · tháng 9, năm 7
>
> **Mục tiêu:** đọc một bài nhận xét có chấm sao và một buổi tập phỏng vấn; nhận ra cách đưa ý kiến có lý do và ví dụ, các cụm mở rộng câu trả lời (Well, I'd say…, The thing is…, To give you an example…, What I've learned is…), giọng đùa trong bài nhận xét (I am being kind), câu tự hỏi tự đáp, và liên hệ chi tiết với các chương trước.

**Từ ngoài bài học** (chưa có trong khoá tới unit này, xem nghĩa để đọc trôi):

| Từ | Nghĩa |

## Bài đọc 1 (Unit 43) — Trevor nhận xét tháng đầu ở đại học

> **An Honest Review of My First Month at College**
>
> Written by: Trevor Carter, for the Westview High "Life After High School" page
>
> Ms. Park asked me to write something for students who are applying to college this year. She said "be honest", so I will be. I am studying environmental engineering at a large public university in Northern California, about eight hours' drive from San Diego. Here is my first-month review.
>
> Classes: four stars out of five.
>
> My first-year engineering class is far harder than anything at high school, and I failed my first quiz. However, the professors hold "office hours", when you can visit them and ask questions, and nobody told me how useful they are. Go to them. Everybody else is too shy, so you will get the professor to yourself.
>
> Living in a dorm: three stars. My roommate is a nice guy from Texas who plays the guitar at midnight. We have had one serious conversation about it. It went better than I expected, partly because I used something that my sister taught me: talk about yourself, not the other person.
>
> Food: two stars, and I am being kind.
>
> I did not appreciate my dad's cooking until I was eating cold pasta for the third night in a row. Dad, if you are reading this: I miss your salmon. Even the broccoli.
>
> Soccer: five stars.
>
> I didn't make the university team, which was disappointing. But I joined a club team, and the players come from twelve different states and three countries. It is the best part of my week.
>
> Overall: four stars.
>
> Is it hard? Yes. Do I sometimes miss home? Every Sunday. Would I choose it again? Absolutely. My advice: choose a place that excites you, not just a place that looks good on paper, and call your family more than you think you need to.

## Bài đọc 2 (Unit 44) — Tracy tập phỏng vấn xin việc với Leah

> **Practicing for the Interview**
>
> Tracy had a final interview on Monday for a full-time job at Brandt & Lowe, the engineering company where she had done her internship. On Saturday, Leah offered to practice with her.
>
> **Leah:** OK. First question: why do you want to work here?
>
> **Tracy:** Because I liked my internship.
>
> **Leah:** That's true, but it's too short. Interviewers want reasons and examples. Try again, and this time, keep going.
>
> **Tracy:** Well, I'd say there are two main reasons. The first is the work itself. During my internship, I found two mistakes in an old bridge report, and my supervisor gave me a bigger task the next day. I realized that I love checking details that other people miss. The second is the people. The thing is, nobody there pretends to know everything. People ask questions, and that makes it a safe place to learn.
>
> **Leah:** Much better. Next: tell me about a weakness.
>
> **Tracy:** Honestly? I'm a perfectionist. To give you an example, before my final exams last year, I was so worried about being perfect that I stopped sleeping properly, and my work got worse, not better. What I've learned since then is to break big tasks into smaller pieces and to ask for feedback early, instead of hiding my work until it's "ready".
>
> **Leah:** That's a good answer, because it's true, and because you showed what you did about it. Also, I remember that phone call.
>
> **Leah:** Last question. Where do you see yourself in five years?
>
> **Tracy:** That's a hard one. I suppose I'd like to be leading small projects, maybe on bridges or water systems. But to be honest, I'd like to keep learning more than I'd like a particular title.
>
> Leah closed her notebook. "You'll be fine. Just remember: answer the question, add a reason, give an example, and then stop. You don't have to fill every silence." On Wednesday, Tracy sent one word to the family chat: "HIRED!"

## 💡 Mẹo làm bài

- Bài nhận xét có chấm điểm: mỗi mục có một con số và lý do. Câu hỏi "Why does he give … stars?" lấy lý do ngay sau con số.
- Câu đùa trong bài nhận xét (`two stars, and I am being kind`) nghĩa là thực tế còn tệ hơn con số.
- Câu trả lời phỏng vấn tốt có 3 phần: trả lời → lý do → ví dụ. Câu hỏi về lời khuyên của Leah lấy đúng công thức này.
- Chi tiết nối với các chương trước (bông cải xanh, cuộc gọi đêm trước kỳ thi) giúp đoán ý ngầm. Hãy nhớ lại câu chuyện cũ của nhân vật.
